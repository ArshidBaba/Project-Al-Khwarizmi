# Contributing to Self

## Rules

1. Preserve failed ideas.
2. Never fake understanding.
3. Define terminology.
4. Distinguish intuition from proof.
5. Revisit old assumptions.
6. Ask better questions.
7. Prefer clarity over complexity.

---

## Anti-Patterns

Avoid:

- pseudo-profundity,
- undefined abstractions,
- vague universal claims,
- excessive metaphor,
- confusing language for understanding.

---

## Research Principle

A beautiful idea that cannot survive precision is not yet understood.
