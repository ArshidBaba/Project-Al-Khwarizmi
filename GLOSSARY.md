# Glossary

## Information

Working definition:
A measurable reduction in uncertainty within a formal system.

Notes:
Different fields use this differently.
Semantic information may not equal Shannon information.

---

## Intelligence

Working definition:
The ability of a system to build predictive, compressive, and adaptive internal models.

---

## Emergence

Working definition:
Higher-order behavior arising from interactions among simpler components.

---

## Consciousness

Status:
Undefined.

Need:
- operational definition,
- measurable criteria,
- computational framing.
