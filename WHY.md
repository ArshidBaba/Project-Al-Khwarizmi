# Why This Exists

## Fundamental Questions

What is computation?

Can intelligence emerge from purely computational systems?

Are there fundamental limits to self-knowledge?

Is consciousness computational?

Can information exist independently of observers?

Are physical laws computational constraints?

---

## Personal Motivation

This project exists to:

- think deeply,
- learn rigorously,
- develop mathematical intuition,
- and explore foundational questions seriously.

The objective is not performative intelligence.

The objective is understanding.

---

## Standards

Ideas should eventually move toward:

- definitions,
- models,
- assumptions,
- formal reasoning,
- and critique.

Speculation is allowed.

But speculation should evolve into structure.

---

## Success Criteria

Success means:

- clearer thinking,
- stronger intuition,
- deeper understanding,
- better questions,
- and more rigorous reasoning.
