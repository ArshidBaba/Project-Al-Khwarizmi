# Project Alkhwarizmi

## Overview

Project Alkhwarizmi is an evolving independent research initiative exploring:

- Computation
- Information
- Consciousness
- Mathematics
- Logic
- Physics
- Emergence
- Intelligence
- Philosophy of mind

The goal is not necessarily publication or novelty.

The goal is:
- deep understanding,
- rigorous exploration,
- synthesis across disciplines,
- and development of original thought.

This repository functions as:
- a research notebook,
- a theoretical laboratory,
- a knowledge graph,
- and a long-term intellectual archive.

---

## Core Themes

- Universal computation
- Computational theories of mind
- Information-theoretic models of reality
- Limits of formal systems
- Emergence and complexity
- Self-reference and consciousness
- Computation in biological systems
- Foundations of mathematics and logic

---

## Guiding Principles

1. Clarity over obscurity.
2. Precision over aesthetics.
3. Formalization where possible.
4. Honest uncertainty.
5. Preserve failed ideas.
6. Think independently first.
7. Compare with literature later.

---

## Long-Term Goal

To build a coherent map of ideas connecting computation, information, intelligence, and reality.
