# Idea Fragments

## Compression as Intelligence

Possible idea:
Intelligence may correlate with the ability to compress environmental structure into predictive internal representations.

Potential connections:
- Kolmogorov complexity
- predictive processing
- minimum description length
- biological adaptation

Questions:
- Can this be formalized?
- Does prediction imply compression?
- Is consciousness necessary for compression?

Need:
