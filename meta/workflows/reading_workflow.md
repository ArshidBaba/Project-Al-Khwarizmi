# Reading Workflow

## Step 1

Read quickly for:
- problem statement,
- intuition,
- high-level structure.

---

## Step 2

Read slowly for:
- definitions,
- assumptions,
- proofs,
- limitations.

---

## Step 3

Write:
- questions,
- critiques,
- confusions,
- possible extensions.

---

## Step 4

Connect ideas to:
- existing notes,
- open problems,
- active investigations.

---

## Step 5

Attempt your own explanation from memory.
